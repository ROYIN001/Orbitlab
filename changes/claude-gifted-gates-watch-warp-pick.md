## CHANGELOG

- Watch (the owner's choice B in chat, 2026-11-06, "ตอบข้อ B เลย แก้ที่แอป"): a warp set from outside the launch viewer, WebMCP's `control_playback` or the `.` and `,` keys, now counts as picking a speed there, as the viewer's own buttons do. Watch leaves Auto, marks the button of the matching speed (none for a warp that is not 1, 5, 25 or 100) and keeps it until Auto is pressed or the next launch. Before, its automatic pace set its own warp again at its next change, and at its first update after a launch when that came after the call, while `control_playback` answered `ok: true` with the new warp and the Auto button stayed marked; that is what made `r3-first-launch` (#209) and `r2-flight-shell` (#212) miss their end card or T+150 on a slow runner. The old WebMCP warp 25 of `r2-flight-shell`'s Watch step took 167.3 and 160.9 s to T+150 with eight busy loops on four cores (limit 120 s, the 25× replaced by 1× within 1.2 s) and now takes 30.8 and 29.9 s with the warp held. A warp set while replaying, or in the workspace, does not change Watch. `index-*.js` +168 B (2,654,168 of 2,655,000), precache code 14,678.7 of 14,679.0 kB, no ceiling raised.

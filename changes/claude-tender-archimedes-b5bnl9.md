Budget ceilings raised by 22.2 kB of code (D-38, approved by the owner in chat, 2026-10-28), and precache code and index-*.js by 1 kB more each for the glow and fairing fixes (approved 2026-10-30).
- Physics (Soyuz Anatomy, the owner's choice in chat, 2026-10-30): the fairing halves start beside the stack. A point-mass flight dropped them once a step had moved the stack to its end and then flew them that whole step as well, so they appeared one step ahead of it (224 m on a crewed Soyuz-2.1a, 616 m on a 2.1b) and were never seen opening; each point-mass piece now flies only the part of the step since it was let go of, as a rigid one already did (`DebrisTracker.stepDebris`). Any other piece let go of after a step has moved the stack now starts beside it too: the rocket a crewed Soyuz leaves behind when its escape fires inside a flight step (range safety, a structural or aerodynamic break-up, an abandoned insertion) starts at the stack, its strap-ons and stages one step nearer than before (up to about 140 m at 0.1 s steps), and those flights' `evt.stageImpact` events come one step later (0.1 s; 0.01 s with six degrees of freedom). No stack flies differently: the stacks and the D01 fleet and rigid fingerprints are unchanged, and so are the strap-ons and stages dropped at staging and the escape's own bodies; every other event log probed is as it was. The halves' own paths do change on every vehicle with a fairing flown as a point mass (landing 30–183 m away from before), and so do those aborts' spent stages, so "the physics is unchanged" above no longer holds for them.

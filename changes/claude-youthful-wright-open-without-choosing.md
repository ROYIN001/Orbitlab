## CHANGELOG

- Learner profiles (owner request, 2026-10-14): Orbitlab can be used without choosing a learner. A new tab, a deleted learner or a learner whose data cannot be read no longer stops at the Learner profiles window over the landing page: the tab opens on the learner whose work was saved last, or on a new, empty **Learner 1** when none can be read. Read-only (another tab has the learner) and visit-only starts are explained by the line under the top bar, not by opening the window. Learner profiles opens by itself only when no learner can be opened or added at all (40 learners, none readable).

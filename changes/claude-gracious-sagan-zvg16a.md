## CHANGELOG

- Replay (bug fix): an escape or a capsule's return (status 'abort') is drawn on its arc between two recorded frames, through the cubic that meets both frames' states, as a six-DOF coast is since #187. It was always drawn on the straight line between its two samples, which sags gΔt²/8 under the arc: over the 10 s frames above the air, Freedom 7 (MR-3) 117 m, 128 m while its retro-rockets fired, and Soyuz 18a's spacecraft 117 m; Vostok-1 4.7 m over its 2 s frames. Each change of an escape's velocity is at a recorded frame (an event's, or a change of body, which holds the earlier frame); what happens inside a span (a retro-rocket burning out, a canopy filling or out of its reefing, the air) changes only its acceleration. Measured on MR-3, Soyuz 18a, MS-10, T-10-1 and Vostok-1, six-DOF and point-mass, against the simulation's own state at every step: 117 m → 0.001 m, 128 m → 2.3 m, never more than 1 cm worse. A spent body beside the escape that fires nothing goes its way at any height, so the two keep their places (the spent Redstone 25 m from Freedom 7 had been drawn 117 m off it once the capsule took the cubic). Vostok's sphere on the ground, waiting for its pilot, keeps the line: it comes to rest a step after its landing event, where the cubic would be 8 m off. Sizes stay under their ceilings.

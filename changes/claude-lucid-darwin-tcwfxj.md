## CHANGELOG

- Debris (bug fix): where a spent stage, booster or fairing came down is given at the moment it touched the ground. Its longitude, in the telemetry panel's spent-stage list, on the 2-D map and in `evt.stageImpact`, was turned with the Earth's angle at the start of the step that brought it down (point mass) or at its end (six-DOF): up to 0.125° off in a 30 s orbit step, and 0.072° (5.2 km) at most on the watch missions (Vostok-1's fairing). Now it is taken at the contact time. The flights are unchanged; 16 point-mass and 4 six-DOF impact events on the watch missions change their longitude in the second decimal, and no fingerprint or golden needed re-recording. Sizes stay under their ceilings.

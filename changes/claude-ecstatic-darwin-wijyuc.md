## CHANGELOG

- Replay (bug fix): a long recording thinned past its frame budget no longer leaves one span of hours in a coast, and a docked spacecraft and its station stay together between two frames. The recorder's last coast pass halved every coast frame each time it ran, so a six-DOF Soyuz MS docked for 12,000 s kept one docked span of 7,981 s, over which the spacecraft was drawn 9,271 km off its orbit and the station, on its own blend, 14,945 km from it; that pass now stops at a frame every ten minutes (the longest docked span 570 s, the spacecraft and the station 3.0 km off, 18 m apart as flown), and the station takes the spacecraft's own blend. Apollo's command module, whose status stays 'orbit' to the water, is recorded every 2 s in the air, as a coast there is, and its entry is no longer thinned with the coast: under its mains it was drawn 202 m off once thinned to a minute, now 0.8 m (point-mass Apollo 11, four hours on the water). Sizes went down (index −5 B, flight worker −7 B).

## CHANGELOG

- Replay (bug fix): a frame handed out by a seek or a replay is now its own copy all the way down, as `cloneFrame` promised. The parts a rocket dropped on the way up, Apollo's state (its Moon figures, plan and the CSM, ascent stage and service module beside it), the place a returning booster is flown to and a six-DOF flight's control-fault record were still the recording's own objects, so a panel that wrote into what it was given would have changed the recording, and for the booster's landing place, the live simulation's guidance. Nothing does that today, and now a seek's frame cannot (a live frame still shares the landing place and the fault record with the simulation; see the report). What a seek shows is unchanged to the bit on all 20 watch missions and a six-DOF fault flight; the two chunks that carry the code are 77–79 B larger each.

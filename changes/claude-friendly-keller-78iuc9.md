## CHANGELOG

- Build (Redesign A, S0; identical output, D-38 offset): unused `.section-*` rules leave `src/ui/modes.css` (−981 B of CSS) and the published page leaves out the indentation at the start of each line of `index.html` (−2.1 kB more for every visitor and in the offline copy); what the page shows and does is unchanged, and this step changes no ceiling.
- Interface (Redesign A, S1; a look change; the owner accepted it on screenshots, 2026-11-14, which do not show the Structure section's icons that came with the rebase): online, the app now uses IBM Plex Sans Thai, IBM Plex Sans (for Russian) and IBM Plex Mono, in the page and in the charts and on-board readouts; Thai text is no longer letter-spaced; the top bar's symbols (sections, levels, menu arrow, help, learner, My work, About, camera sequence, Lessons) are line icons, and so are the marks of the two links into the Structure section; the Lessons button is a mint outline instead of gold. Offline fonts are the system's, as before.
- Watch (Redesign A, S2; a look change; the owner accepted it on screenshots, 2026-11-14, which do not show the Structure section, added by the rebase): the scene comes first. The mission time, altitude and speed are one small clock block at the top centre (on a phone, its own row under the title), what is happening is one caption card at the bottom left above the controls, the parking-orbit note sits just above that caption (in a short or narrow scene at the top centre, as before), and nothing covers the middle of the scene; the dark band across the bottom is gone, and the Moon note has the caption's dark backing. In a short scene the caption is one line, its sentence cut short. A lesson's strip no longer shows in Watch, on the landing page or in the Structure section.
- Build (Redesign A, S3 offset; identical output, D-38 offset): three declarations in `src/style.css` that a later rule always overrode are gone (−99 B of CSS); nothing the app shows or does changes, and this step changes no ceiling.
- Lessons (Redesign A, S3; a look change; the owner accepted it on screenshots, 2026-11-14): studying a lesson is light, like a classroom: the lesson catalogue, the placement test and a lesson's strip over the flight have white cards, dark ink and a blue accent in place of the gold on dark. The 3-D scene, the top bar and the test's figures stay dark, and so do the teacher pages (worksheets, writing a lesson, checking results). The test's recommended-path legend no longer calls the start chip gold ("★: start here"); it is blue now.

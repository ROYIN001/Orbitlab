  6. Why is a camera satellite put in a sun-synchronous orbit?

    • a) It stands still over one spot.
    • b) It needs no fuel to stay up.
    • c) It is always in the Earth's shadow, which keeps the camera cool.
    • d) Each place is seen under much the same sunlight on every pass, so pictures can be compared.

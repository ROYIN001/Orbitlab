Answer key — Case sheet: THEOS-2 over Bangkok

Thailand's Earth-observation satellite in its sun-synchronous orbit

Name:Code:CASE-THEOS2

Questions

  1. 0.9856 °/d (± 0.0005 °/d)

    One turn a year: 360° / 365.2422 days.

  2. 0.991 °/d (± 0.010 °/d)

    dΩ/dt = −(3/2) J₂ (R/p)² n cos i, p = a(1 − e²), n = √(μ/a³); in degrees per day. Within about half a per cent of the rate wanted: the other terms and the set's own fit make up the rest.

  3. 621 km (± 2 km)

    a − R, with R the equatorial radius.

  4. 656 km (± 10 km)

    On a sphere of R_mean: the Earth-centre angle is asin((R_mean + h)/R_mean · sin 45°) − 45°, and the distance that angle times R_mean.

  5. 10.03 h (± 0.02 h)

    UTC + longitude / 15° per hour: 3.333 + 100.502 / 15; at the published local time, as a morning orbit must be.

  6. d) Each place is seen under much the same sunlight on every pass, so pictures can be compared.

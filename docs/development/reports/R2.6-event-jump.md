### The menu

- It is the timeline's event chooser (R2.4), opened from the opener instead of
  a cluster chip: a list with `role="menu"`, named by `tl.jump.label`, over
  the page.
- It lists `quickEvents()`: the earliest recorded event of each of seven
  kinds, in time order. The kinds: liftoff; Max-Q; staging (a booster or a
  stage separation); fairing; insertion (the five `INSERTION` keys); landing
  (eleven landing, splashdown and touchdown keys); failure (any event of
  severity `fail`). Every kind present is listed, on every screen. Each item
  shows the event's T+ time and its short label. Its title is `tl.jump.title`
  ("Go to 5 s before “Max Q”"). Each item is at least 44 px tall.
- Only recorded events are listed (D-39). The opener and an open menu are
  refilled only when the list's `kind@t` signature changes, after a reset and
  on a language change; never per frame.
- Choosing an item seeks to 5 s before the event (`App.seekToEvent`), in
  replay. A jump does not choose a view. A view you picked stays. With
  Cinematic on, the camera programme shows its own view for the phase you land
  in.
- The menu marks the item whose jump lands at or before the cursor
  (`aria-current="time"`), and opens with the focus on it. So after a jump it
  opens on the event just jumped to, not the one before. It reads the app's
  cursor through a getter (`cursor` in `TimelineCallbacks`), not the cursor
  the timeline last drew, which is a frame old right after a seek.
- It opens above or below its opener, on the side with more room, never over
  it, where a second press would choose an item. Its height is capped to that
  room and the list scrolls. A focused item is scrolled into view.
- Keys. Enter or Space on the opener opens the menu. In the menu, Up and Down
  move and wrap at the ends; Home and End go to the first and the last item;
  Enter or Space choose; Escape and Tab close it, and the focus goes back to
  the opener. A choice made with a key gives the focus back to the opener. A
  choice made with the pointer leaves it on no button, so Space then plays
  instead of opening the menu again. After a jump the arrow keys seek 5 s
  (30 s with Shift) from the app's cursor. A press on the opener while the
  menu is open closes it; so does a press outside it.

## Fonts: the container's Inter and the CI runners' DejaVu Sans

The fit depends on the width of words, so its results depend on the font.
Here `system-ui` and `sans-serif` resolve to Inter. The CI runners' font is
DejaVu Sans. The house practice, recorded in
[revert-drill-GK1.md](revert-drill-GK1.md) ("Browser"), runs a journey with
the CI runners' font through a fontconfig file (`FONTCONFIG_FILE`) that maps
`system-ui` and `sans-serif` to DejaVu Sans and rejects Inter. With it,
`fc-match system-ui` gives DejaVu Sans; without it, Inter. Every journey
result in this report names its font. Liberation Sans, with Arial's metrics,
was measured too, where a word's width decides a line. Two of this PR's
journeys failed in the CI runners' font on a prototype build of this design.

changes they pass in it (below, "Checks at this PR's head", which also gives

soyuz-ms-27-nasa.mp3
  NASA TV coverage of the launch of Soyuz MS-27 (NASA astronaut Jonny Kim,
  Roscosmos cosmonauts Sergey Ryzhikov and Alexey Zubritsky), Baikonur
  Cosmodrome, 8 April 2025: 600 s from T-60 s to T+540 s, mono, 48 kbit/s.
  Source: NASA Image and Video Library, video
  iss073m260980444_NASA_Astronaut_Jonny_Kim_Soyuz_MS-27_Launch_250408
  (https://images.nasa.gov). NASA material is not protected by copyright in
  the United States (NASA Images and Media Usage Guidelines); the broadcast
  carries the Roscosmos launch-control loop under NASA's commentary.
  T-0 (liftoff) is at 60.0 s in this file.

ru-calls/*.mp3
  Russian launch control's calls, cut from two recordings and levelled
  (high-pass 140 Hz, loudness -18 LUFS, mono 64 kbit/s):
  - kontakt-podyoma.mp3 ("Контакт подъёма есть"): the Ministry of Defence of
    the Russian Federation, launch of a Soyuz-2.1b from the Plesetsk
    cosmodrome, 16 March 2020 (mil.ru), CC BY 4.0. Wikimedia Commons:
    File:Soyuz-2.1b launch from the Plesetsk cosmodrome (16-03-2020).webm,
    33.0-35.6 s.
  - t170.mp3, t180.mp3, t190.mp3 (the flight reports at T+170, 180 and 190 s),
    orbit.mp3 ("…выведен на орбиту. Репортаж окончен."): the Presidential
    Executive Office (kremlin.ru), first launch from the Vostochny
    cosmodrome, Soyuz-2.1a, 28 April 2016, CC BY 4.0. Wikimedia Commons:
    File:Президент России — 2016-04-28 — С космодрома Восточный произведён
    запуск ракеты-носителя «Союз-2˙1а».webm, 73.35-75.95 s, 82.85-86.95 s,
    93.4-97.75 s and 154.55-157.95 s.

falcon9-crew-12-pad-nasa.mp3
  NASA's pad microphone ("Blast", the right channel of the Tech Feed / Blast
  recording) at the launch of NASA's SpaceX Crew-12 on a Falcon 9 from Space
  Launch Complex 40, Cape Canaveral Space Force Station, 13 February 2026:
  162 s from T-12 s to T+150 s, mono, 64 kbit/s; no voice. Source: Wikimedia
  Commons, File:NASA's SpaceX Crew-12 Live Launch Coverage - Tech Feed (L)
  Blast (R) (KSC-20260213-AU-LMM01-0001-SpaceX Crew 12 Live Launch Coverage
  Tech Feed Blast-M19013).mp3, 7329.6-7491.6 s, public domain (NASA). T-0
  is at 12.0 s in this file, from the count on the left channel ("… two,
  one, ignition, liftoff" at 7339.7-7343.5 s; ±0.5 s).
